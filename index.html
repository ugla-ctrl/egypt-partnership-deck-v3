The momentI

On 2 August 2027 the Sun goes out over the City of the Sun.

6:23of totality over Luxor
87years until a longer one on land
10days, Aswan to Giza

Luxor was Thebes, capital of the New Kingdom, a city built to align daily life with the sky. We watch from the West Bank, among the Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut and the Colossi of Memnon, with the Nile between us and Karnak. It is the single most photographed place on Earth to be standing in a shadow that long.

Around it: a week on the river from Aswan, a New Moon ceremony the evening before, music into the night after, and three days at Giza ending with the Lion’s Gate at the Sphinx on 8 August. Everyone who comes is there for the same six minutes.

IV
The experience · I · 1 AugustV

The eve: every pathway, together for the first time.

At dusk on the last night before the eclipse, the ships and the hotels meet on the West Bank for the New Moon Ceremony. Elders light the first candles and open the circle, and a community that has gathered since Iceland finds itself again. Then music carries the night.

Artist’s impression.

The experience · II · 2 AugustVI

Eclipse day: a festival built around six minutes.

From 11:00 the grounds beside the Marsam open: shade and colour, art and installations against the rustic West Bank. The Moon touches the Sun at 11:40. At 13:01 it covers it, and a thousand people, every one in eclipse glasses, go still.

11:00 grounds open · 11:40 first contact · 13:01 totality

Artist’s impression.

The experience · III · 2 to 3 AugustVII

The feast, then the celebration.

After totality everyone retires into the shade by the Marsam: long tables, Egyptian food, Saidi and Nubian musicians, elders and guests at one table. Then the release, a DJ and a full audio-visual celebration of the longest eclipse of the century, through the night to a send-off on the morning of the 3rd.
