Egypt EclipseThe Moment
The invitation

On 2 August 2027 the Sun goes out over the City of the Sun.

6:23of totality over Luxor
87 yearsuntil a longer one on land
10 daysAswan to Luxor to Giza

Luxor was Thebes, capital of the New Kingdom, a city built to align daily life with the sky. We watch from the West Bank, among the Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut and the Colossi of Memnon, with the Nile between us and Karnak. It is the single most photographed place on Earth to be standing in a shadow that long.

Around it: a week on the river from Aswan, a New Moon ceremony the evening before, music into the night after, and three days at Giza ending with the Lion’s Gate at the Sphinx on 8 August. Everyone who comes is there for the same six minutes.

The Partnership · 2027
Egypt EclipseThe Experience · I
1 August

The eve: every pathway, together for the first time.

At dusk on the last night before the eclipse, the ships and the hotels meet on the West Bank for the New Moon Ceremony. Local wisdom keepers and elders from many lands light the first candles and open the circle, and a community that has been gathering since Iceland finds itself again. Then music carries the night.

Artist’s impressions. Partner moments: the welcome at the circle, and the concert.

Egypt EclipseThe Experience · II
2 August

Eclipse day: a festival built around six minutes.

From 11:00 the grounds beside the Marsam open as a gathering made with an eye: shade and colour, art and installations, set against the rustic West Bank. The Moon first touches the Sun at 11:40. At 13:01 it covers it, and a thousand people, every one in eclipse glasses, go still.

11:00 grounds open · 11:40 first contact · 13:01 totality · the afternoon in the shade

Artist’s impressions. Partner moments: the grounds, the glasses, the countdown and totality itself.

Egypt EclipseThe Experience · III
2 to 3 August

The feast, then the celebration.

After totality everyone retires into the shade by the Marsam: long tables, Egyptian food, Saidi and Nubian musicians, elders and guests at the same table. Then the release, a DJ and a full audio-visual celebration of the longest eclipse of the century, through the night until a send-off together on the morning of the 3rd.

Artist’s impressions. Partner moments: the feast, and the night’s celebration.
