# Egypt Eclipse 2027 · The Partnership — a flip-through magazine

A luxury flip-through magazine on papyrus. A photographic magazine cover opens to editorial
pages — masthead headers, kickers, two-column justified body, stat rows, pull facts, captioned
plates and folios — printed on an aged papyrus sheet. Same content as the deck (v1 / v2), as a
magazine.

## Design
- Magazine cover: full-bleed eclipse-over-Luxor hero, EGYPT ECLIPSE masthead, cover lines.
- Interior: professional editorial layout on real generated papyrus (ElevenLabs Seedream); tighter
  magazine type; one source slide per page (20 leaves incl. covers, close to the original).
- Living painted plates: our Nile-to-Pyramids painterly clips play inside gold frames; the eve,
  eclipse day and the night are full-bleed photo features.
- Flip with StPageFlip (single upright page, realistic turn). Only the on-screen page's clip plays;
  reduced-motion / data-saver fall back to stills.

## Content
100% to the source copy, figures, prices and contacts. (Mitch's public contact shows as
mitch@imxp.org, his standing public-deck address.)

## Build
Single index.html (generated). StPageFlip from cdn.jsdelivr.net; fonts from Google. Generated art
in media/ (papyrus.webp), painterly clips in media/clips/, photos and logos in media/.
