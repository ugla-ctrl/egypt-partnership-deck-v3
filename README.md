# Egypt Eclipse 2027 · The Partnership Book (v3)

A vertical, luxurious flip-through book. The closed cover sits as a 3D carved-Egyptian tome in a
candlelit frame; tap the edge and it flattens open to a single upright papyrus page you turn, one
leaf at a time. Same content as the deck (v1 / v2), redesigned as an illuminated manuscript.

## The idea
- Closed book reads as a 3D object: tilted, with page-edge thickness and a spine.
- Carved stone cover with a bronze scroll-rod, a gold medallion (starburst + octagon + eclipse disc),
  a cartouche panel and scarabs — inspired by an antique Egyptian tome, drawn fresh so it is our own.
- Aged, crinkled papyrus pages with a winged-sun watermark, a lotus frieze, gold rules, drop caps,
  and roman-numeral folios.
- Living painted plates: our own Nile-to-Pyramids painterly animations play inside gold frames
  (the Nile flight opens the journey; Giza closes it; the Luxor balloons, Luxor temples, the Sphinx
  at night and desert b-roll as moving plates). Only the page on screen plays; a still poster shows
  otherwise; reduced-motion / data-saver fall back to stills.

## Content
Every slide of the source deck, kept 100% to the source copy, figures, prices and contacts.
Dense slides are split across continuation leaves rather than compressed. 26 leaves in all.
(Mitch's public contact shows as mitch@imxp.org, his standing public-deck address.)

## Build
Single `index.html` (generated). StPageFlip from cdn.jsdelivr.net; fonts from Google. Painterly
clips in `media/clips/` (seamless loops, 1280 and 640, with posters); photos and logos in `media/`.
