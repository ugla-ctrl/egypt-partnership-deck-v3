# Egypt Eclipse 2027 · The Partnership Book (v3)

A vertical, luxurious flip-through book: a gilded ancient-Egyptian cover that opens to a single
upright papyrus page you turn, one leaf at a time. Same content as the partnership deck
(v1 https://ugla-ctrl.github.io/egypt-partnership-deck/ , v2
https://ugla-ctrl.github.io/egypt-partnership-deck-v2/ ), redesigned as an illuminated manuscript.

## The idea
- A closed gilded tome in the middle of a dark, candlelit frame. Tap the edge to open.
- One portrait page at a time with a realistic page-turn (StPageFlip), never a two-page spread.
- Aged, crinkled papyrus pages, gold rules and folios, drop caps, an illuminated-manuscript hand.
- Living painted plates: our own Nile-to-Pyramids painterly animations play inside gold frames
  (the Nile flight opens the journey; Giza closes it; the Luxor balloons, Luxor temples, the
  Sphinx at night and desert b-roll sit as moving plates). Only the page on screen plays; a still
  poster shows otherwise, and reduced-motion / data-saver fall back to stills.

## Content
Cover · Title · The moment · The room · The journey · How it works (seven pathways) · The eve ·
Eclipse day · The night · Proof · Why brands · Presenting partner ($400,000) · Totality at Luxor
($150,000) · Lion's Gate at the Sphinx ($150,000) · Category partners ($50,000 to $100,000) ·
Hosted presence · Content & rights · The platform · Behind the gathering · Next step · Back cover.
All copy, figures, prices and contacts are kept to the source.

## Build
Single `index.html`. StPageFlip from cdn.jsdelivr.net; fonts from Google. Painterly clips in
`media/clips/` (seamless loops at 1280 and 640, with posters), photos and logos in `media/`.
